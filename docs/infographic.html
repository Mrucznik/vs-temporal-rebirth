TEMPORAL REBIRTH

Hardcore lives · ghosts · a way back

1ALIVE

Hardcore world: you start with the world's player lives.

Hold a temporal gear on the ground, as when setting your spawn point: +1 life.

2DEATH

Each death costs a life. On your last one, you do not respawn.

With Downed: being downed is not death. Only bleeding out, giving up or execution counts.

3GHOST

You linger as a spectator, free to watch, unable to act.

Bound to where you fell: drift past the tether and you are pulled back.

4RETURN

The next day (06:00 server time) you wake at your spawn point.

Or sooner: a living friend holds a temporal gear beside a rift. The gear shatters and you return at their side. Ghosts must be online.

COMMANDS
/rebirth lives · /rebirth ghosts · /rebirth target <ghost> (pick whom the ritual brings back) · /rebirth revive <player> (admin)
CONFIGURABLE
lives per gear · return time or off · tether radius · ritual range
