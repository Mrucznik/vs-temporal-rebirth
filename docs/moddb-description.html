Death should matter. It shouldn't be the end.

Temporal Rebirth turns Vintage Story's hardcore lives into a cycle: earn lives with temporal gears, haunt the place where you fell when you run out, and come back the next day — or sooner, if a friend performs a ritual at a rift.

How it works

  • Earn lives. Use a temporal gear the usual way (hold right click on the ground, as when setting your spawn point) and gain an extra life.
  • Last life. Each death costs a life. When you have none left, you don't respawn.
  • Become a ghost. You stay in the world as a spectator, bound to the place where you died. Wander too far and you are pulled back.
  • Return. The next morning you wake up at your spawn point. Or a living friend can bring you back sooner: they hold a temporal gear beside an active rift, the gear shatters, and you return at their side.

If several ghosts are waiting, the friend chooses whom to bring back with /rebirth target <name>. Only ghosts who are online can be revived.

Compatibility

  • Install on both client and server.
  • Downed: works together. Being downed is not death; only bleeding out, giving up or being executed costs a life.
  • Temporal Lives: do not use both. They change the same game code; Temporal Rebirth already includes earning lives from gears.

Server setup

The mod only does something on worlds with limited lives. Pick the number of lives when creating the world, or on an existing world run:

/worldconfig playerlives 3

then restart the server. Rifts must be enabled for the ritual (they are by default).

Config

File: ModConfig/temporalrebirth.json on the server.

  • LivesPerGear (default 1) — lives gained per temporal gear. -1 uses the world's temporalGearRespawnUses.
  • ReturnMode (default NextDay) — NextDay, Hours or Disabled (ritual only).
  • NextDayResetHour (default 6) — hour of the server's local time when ghosts from the previous day return.
  • ReturnAfterHours (default 24) — waiting time when ReturnMode is Hours.
  • GhostNoClip (default false) — false: ghosts fly but cannot pass through blocks; true: they pass through walls like a vanilla spectator.
  • GhostTetherRadius (default 24) — how far a ghost may drift from where it died, in blocks. 0 = no limit.
  • RitualRiftRange (default 8) — how close to a rift the friend must stand, in blocks.
  • RitualReviveAt (default Caster) — where the ritual brings the ghost back: Caster (next to the friend; can be used to travel), DeathSpot or Spawn.
  • RitualCooldownHours (default 0) — hours before the same player can be revived by a ritual again. 0 = no limit.
  • ReturnHealthPercent, ReturnSatietyPercent (default 100) — health and satiety after coming back, in percent.

Commands

  • /rebirth lives — how many lives you have left
  • /rebirth ghosts — who is waiting and when they come back
  • /rebirth target <ghost> — choose whom your next ritual brings back
  • /rebirth revive <player> — admin: bring a ghost back where they stand
